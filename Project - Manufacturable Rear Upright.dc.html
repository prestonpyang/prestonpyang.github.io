BAJA SAE Rear Upright DFM Redesign

Redesigned the right rear upright to eliminate non-functional geometry, simplifying the bore contour and standardizing all fillets and chamfers, with the bearing bore and mounting positions held to print.

Role
Design Engineer, CAD & DFM
Timeline
One build season, FSAE
Tools
SolidWorks, CNC mill & lathe
Status
Machined and installed
Drawing sheet, Rev 1, dimensioned and toleranced per ASME Y14.5
Outcome

A DFM revision that cut machining time out of the rear upright without moving a single functional interface. Every fillet was standardized to an available end-mill radius, the non-functional bore contour was removed, and the 2.678 in bearing bore stayed at +0.002 / -0.000 in. Drawing toleranced per ASME Y14.5, part machined in 7075-T6 and running on the car.

The upright locates the wheel bearing and suspension pickups, so the bore and hole pattern were fixed inputs. The prior revision carried a non-functional contour around the main bore and an unsymmetric fillet at the tie-rod mount. Both added machining time without adding stiffness.

My contribution
  • Reviewed the part feature by feature to separate functional from decorative geometry
  • Revised the CAD, holding bore diameter, tolerance, and hole locations unchanged
  • Standardized fillet radii to the shop’s available end-mill sizes
  • Dimensioned and toleranced the drawing per ASME Y14.5 and handed it to the machine shop
Objective & Constraints
Bearing bore
2.678 in bore held to +0.002 / -0.000 in for a press-fit wheel bearing, unchanged
Manufacturability
Remove non-functional geometry, contours, and one-off fillets that add machining time without adding stiffness
Tolerancing
±0.005 in two-place decimals, GD&T per ASME Y14.5, dimensions in inches
What Changed

Bore diameter, tolerance, and hole locations are unchanged from the prior revision. The scope was limited to non-functional geometry: features simplified where possible, and fillets and chamfers standardized to reduce tooling changeovers.

  • Simplified the complex contour around the main bore down to what the bearing actually needs
  • Removed the unsymmetric fillet near the tie-rod mount and replaced it with a nominal, symmetric radius
  • Standardized every fillet on the part to nominal radii matched to the shop's available end-mill sizes, instead of one-off radii
  • Removed chamfers that weren't doing anything functionally
  • Turned the inner bore chamfer into a fillet for better tool accessibility during machining

Reflection
  • This was subtraction, not redesign. Holding the bore and hole pattern fixed made the revision fast to review and approve.
  • Standardizing fillets to available end-mill radii cut tooling changeovers without changing part stiffness.
  • Turning the inner bore chamfer into a fillet only came up because I checked how the cutter reaches the feature.
  • The deliverable was a toleranced drawing, not just a CAD model. The shop needs both.
